`26 Oct 14:03 PKT` · pehli baar dekhi `26 Oct 16:11` · investinglive

Speeches and interviews can change expectations for future monetary policy, affecting bonds, currencies, equities and so on. To limit these signals immediately before policy decisions, many central banks have a blackout period. A blackout period is a predefined period during which policymakers are restricted from publicly discussing monetary policy or the upcoming decision.
